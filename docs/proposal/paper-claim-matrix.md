A fixed-answer margin is a score, not by itself a generated decision; a transported Jacobian-lens readout is a representation interpretation, not a causal path or chain-of-thought.

## A source-to-target transplant generation study is optional, not an immediate gap

The repository already has generation-level boundary evidence: Evidence-insensitivity shows a state patch can move a fixed score without a flip, and Selective-intervention M6-V2 reports zero greedy sell→buy flips for held-out L15 k=8 **removal**. These results support C4 without another run.

A separate protocol would be warranted only if the paper needs a behavioural statement about the **source→target L15 k=8 transplant** that established C3's fixed-margin sufficiency. That study would be a new operation, not a replication of M6-V2: it would need a frozen held-out population, source-target pairing, clean/random/final-position/self-source controls, greedy parser, paired flip estimand and uncertainty. Its result could establish or limit behaviour for that transplant, but would not complete cross-model/task generalisation, certify a selective intervention, or test the non-orthogonal downstream projection hypothesis.

## Claims the paper must not make without later evidence

- Do not describe the depth-wise transfer or L15 subspace as a discrete reasoning path, chain-of-thought, or complete causal circuit.
- Do not call the entity-induced decision gap harmful bias without a separate normative definition and evaluation.
- Do not call a single neuron an entity concept, a complete entity cell, or an entity-specific decision direction based on factual disruption, calibration, attribution or a margin response alone.
- Do not describe fixed-answer margin movement as a behavioural decision change unless the owning protocol reports actual generated decision flips.
- Do not claim generality across models, model scales or non-financial tasks before their independent protocols and results exist.

## Evidence ownership

This matrix is a navigation and claim-boundary document. Numerical values, experimental populations, controls, gates and provenance remain owned by the linked reports and their `details/` protocols. When a later experiment closes a listed gap, update this matrix by linking to its canonical report; do not retrospectively edit frozen results or promote discovery into confirmation.
